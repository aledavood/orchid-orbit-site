Orchid Orbit

Google connection privacy notice

Effective date: 21 October 2026

This notice covers Orchid Orbit's Google Analytics and Search Console connection. It does not describe the separate CRM, sales or other enterprise data connections available in Orbit.

Data accessed and purpose

With the administrator's Google consent, Orbit reads Analytics report metrics, dimensions and property metadata, and Search Console performance, indexing and sitemap information. Depending on the question, this can include traffic counts, campaign labels, page URLs, events, countries, device categories and search queries. Orbit uses these reports to answer authorized users' questions, compare periods and produce visualizations.

The Google scopes are analytics.readonly and webmasters.readonly. Although these scopes can cover resources accessible to the connected account, Orbit's backend limits this connection to Spartina.ir, Orchidlife.org and GLP1Life.ir. This connection does not read Gmail, Drive files, private form submissions, or individual visitor names and phone numbers. Report labels or URLs may nevertheless contain information supplied by the website's tracking configuration.

Processing and disclosure

Google supplies the reports. Relevant question text, report data and conversation context are processed through OpenAI Codex to prepare answers. Answers and requested chart images are delivered through Telegram to the requesting, authorized user. The administrator can review recorded questions, answers and ratings for support and quality improvement. Website access is granted separately from general bot access.

Orbit does not sell Google data, use it for advertising targeting, or intentionally use it to train a general-purpose AI model. OpenAI and Telegram also process submitted content under their applicable terms and account settings; this notice does not promise that those providers retain no data.

Storage and security

Google authorization credentials are encrypted using Windows account-bound protection and restricted file permissions on the operator's server. Credentials are kept outside the source repository and are not provided to the AI model or Telegram. Reports and derived evidence may be retained in Orbit's local working state for follow-up questions and charts. Questions, final answers, Telegram user identifiers, timestamps, timings, delivery status and ratings are recorded for operational review.

Retention and deletion

The current deployment has no automatic fixed retention period for recorded questions and answers. They remain until the administrator removes them. Contact the administrator to request access to or deletion of relevant saved records and report artifacts. Deleting local records does not itself delete messages already delivered through Telegram or copies retained by service providers; those are subject to their own controls and policies.

Disconnecting

The administrator can disconnect Google in Orbit's local connection setup. This removes the locally stored grant and stops new report requests through that connection. Google-side permission can also be revoked in the connected Google account's third-party connections settings. Disconnecting does not automatically erase existing answers or report artifacts.

Limited use

Orchid Orbit's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

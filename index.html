Orchid Orbit is a private, administrator-controlled reporting assistant. Its website mode answers questions about traffic, campaigns, pages, tracked events and Google Search performance, and can produce charts.

What the Google connection does

The administrator connects a Google account using read-only Google Analytics and Search Console permissions. Orbit restricts reports to Spartina.ir, Orchidlife.org and GLP1Life.ir. The connection cannot change website content, Analytics settings or Search Console settings.

Who can use it

Only Telegram users approved by the administrator and separately granted website-reporting access can request these reports. The application is not a public sign-up service. Other Orbit data sources have separate permissions.

How answers are produced

Orbit retrieves relevant reports from Google, uses OpenAI Codex to analyze them, and sends answers or charts through Telegram. Google's reporting limitations still apply. Activity and tracked events do not by themselves prove sales, patient acquisition or marketing causality.
